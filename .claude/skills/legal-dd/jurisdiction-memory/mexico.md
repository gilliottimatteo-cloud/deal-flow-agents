# Mexico: legal DD document set (subsidiary)

Starting checklist to confirm with local counsel, not legal advice.

Typical role: operating subsidiary. Entity types: **S.A.P.I. de C.V.** (common for startups), **S.A. de C.V.**, **S. de R.L. de C.V.**, and the simplified **S.A.S.**

## Existence and good standing
- Acta constitutiva (notarized deed of incorporation), registered with the Registro Público de Comercio, with its folio mercantil (essential).
- Notarized powers of attorney (poderes) for the legal representative (essential).
- Protocolized minutes of key shareholder meetings and bylaw amendments (essential).

## Ownership
- Share register (libro de registro de acciones) or, for an S. de R.L., the partners' register, showing the parent as owner (essential).
- Foreign investment registration (Registro Nacional de Inversiones Extranjeras) when the parent is foreign (essential).
- Beneficial owner (beneficiario controlador) records (optional).

## Tax
- RFC and constancia de situación fiscal from the SAT (essential).
- Opinión de cumplimiento, the SAT's tax compliance opinion (essential).
- Latest annual tax return (optional).

## Encumbrances and litigation
- Search of the Registro Único de Garantías Mobiliarias (RUG) for movable-asset liens (essential).
- Court and labor-tribunal searches (optional).

## IP and data
- Trademark registration or application with the IMPI (optional).
- Privacy notice (aviso de privacidad) under the federal personal data law (essential if it processes personal data).

## Common pitfalls
- Most entity types need two shareholders, so a local person often holds one share. Document how it ties to the parent.
- Share register not updated after transfers.
- Fintech activity may need authorization from the CNBV. Get written confirmation of regulatory status.
