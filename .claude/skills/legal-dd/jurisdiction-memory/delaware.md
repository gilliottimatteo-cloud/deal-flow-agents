# Delaware (United States): legal DD document set

Starting checklist to confirm with local counsel, not legal advice.

Typical role: holding company, or the intermediate entity under a Cayman holding. Entity types: **C corporation** (most common for venture-backed startups) or **LLC**.

## Existence and good standing
- Certificate of Incorporation (corporation) or Certificate of Formation (LLC), as filed with the Delaware Division of Corporations, with every amendment (essential).
- Certificate of Good Standing, recently issued (essential).
- Franchise tax and annual report status (essential).

## Governance and authority
- Bylaws (corporation) or LLC Agreement (LLC), as amended (essential).
- Board and stockholder consents approving the SAFEs, the option plan and officer appointments (essential).
- Registered agent details (optional).

## Ownership
- Stock ledger or member register, and the fully diluted cap table (essential).
- Founder stock purchase agreements, vesting terms and proof of 83(b) elections (essential).

## Tax
- IRS letter confirming the EIN (essential).
- Latest federal tax return (optional at SAFE stage).

## Encumbrances and litigation
- UCC lien search with the Delaware Department of State (essential).
- Litigation search: Court of Chancery and federal courts (optional).

## IP
- Invention and IP assignment agreements signed by every founder, employee and contractor (essential).

## Common pitfalls
- A founder's IP never assigned to the company.
- Missing 83(b) elections.
- A franchise tax bill on the default method can look alarming; ask counsel about the alternative method.
- A foreign-owned single-member LLC has extra federal reporting duties (Form 5472). Check it was filed.
