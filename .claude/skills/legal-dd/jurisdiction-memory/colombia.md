# Colombia: legal DD document set (subsidiary)

Starting checklist to confirm with local counsel, not legal advice.

Typical role: operating subsidiary. Entity type: **S.A.S.** (sociedad por acciones simplificada).

## Existence and good standing
- Certificado de existencia y representación legal from the Cámara de Comercio, recently issued (essential). It names the legal representative and lists registered embargoes.
- Estatutos (bylaws), as amended (essential).
- Minutes of key shareholder and board meetings (optional).

## Ownership
- Libro de registro de accionistas, registered with the Cámara de Comercio, showing the parent as shareholder (essential).
- Foreign investment registration with the Banco de la República when the parent is foreign (essential).

## Tax
- RUT (tax registration, with the NIT) from the DIAN (essential).
- Latest income tax return (optional).

## Encumbrances and litigation
- Embargoes and measures noted on the Cámara de Comercio certificate (essential).
- Search of the movable-asset guarantees registry (Registro de Garantías Mobiliarias) (essential).
- Court search on the Rama Judicial case lookup (optional).

## IP and data
- Trademark registration or application with the SIC (optional).
- Data-protection compliance under the habeas data rules: privacy policy and, where required, registration of databases with the SIC's national database registry (essential if it processes personal or credit data).

## Common pitfalls
- Share register out of date or never registered, so it does not show the parent.
- Missing foreign investment registration.
- A stale existence certificate. Ask for a fresh one before close.
- Lending or payments activity may fall under the Superintendencia Financiera. Get written confirmation of regulatory status.
