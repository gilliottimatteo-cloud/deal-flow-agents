# Cayman Islands: legal DD document set

Starting checklist to confirm with local counsel, not legal advice.

Typical role: top holding company in a "sandwich" over a Delaware entity and the local operating companies, or directly over the operating companies. Entity type: **exempted company** limited by shares.

## Existence and good standing
- Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Companies (essential).
- Certificate of Good Standing, recently issued (essential).
- Registered office confirmation from the corporate service provider (optional).

## Governance and authority
- Memorandum and Articles of Association, as amended (essential).
- Register of Directors and Officers (essential).
- Director and shareholder resolutions approving the SAFEs and any share issues (essential).

## Ownership
- Register of Members, which is the primary evidence of who holds the shares (essential).
- Share certificates, if issued (optional).
- Beneficial ownership filing status, through the corporate service provider (optional).

## Encumbrances and litigation
- Register of Mortgages and Charges, kept at the registered office (essential).
- Grand Court litigation search (optional).

## Regulatory
- Economic substance notification or filing status (essential).

## Common pitfalls
- The Register of Members not updated after a share issue or transfer, so it does not match the cap table.
- Share classes in the Articles that do not fit how the SAFEs will convert.
- Good standing lost because annual returns or fees lapsed with the service provider.
- In a sandwich, the intermediate entity's own documents forgotten. Build its section from `delaware.md`.
