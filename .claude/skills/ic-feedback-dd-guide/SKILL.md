# IC feedback and DD guide method

You write ONE document that combines both parts, never two separate ones. It opens with the IC feedback. When the decision is PROCEED or REVISIT, the DD guide comes right after it in the SAME document. When the decision is REJECT, the document has no DD guide section whatsoever.

## Section 1: IC feedback (always present)

- Give the decision in plain terms: PROCEED, REVISIT (with the condition for revisiting), or REJECT. Per house-style, it appears straight after the document header.
- Capture the concerns and questions IC members raised in the discussion. Because the discussion has no fixed format, write it down accurately and even-handedly, tying points to themes instead of imposing a rigid structure. Where it fits naturally, cluster points loosely under themes (management, market, competition, financial, legal and regulatory, other).
- If REJECT: state the main reasons in plain words and STOP. No DD guide is written.
- If PROCEED or REVISIT: move on to Section 2.

## Section 2: DD guide (only if PROCEED or REVISIT)

Write the DD guide as a SYNTHESIS, never as a transcription. It draws on two streams of open questions:
1. Questions, flags and gaps that the earlier documents on this company have ALREADY raised: the open questions and flagged uncertainties in the market analysis and the competition analysis, and the per-dimension gaps, flags and data gaps in the Pre-IC scorecard.
2. The new concerns the IC raised in Section 1.

Then:
- Combine both streams and REMOVE anything duplicated or nearly duplicated. When the IC repeated a flag that the analysis had already raised, merge them into one item and note that both raised it.
- Organize the combined set by AREA: Management, Market, Competition, Financial, Legal and regulatory, Other.
- For each item, state the open question, the reason it matters, and what would settle it (the exact evidence, document or call required). Mark priority (HIGH or MEDIUM) where useful. A table per area works well.
- Mark every item OPEN, per house-style, so later stages can mark it RESOLVED.
- The result is one validation plan the DD team works through. No earlier flag may be dropped.

## Output and quality bar

- Internal working document that drives the DD stage.
- ONE Markdown file, formatted per the house-style skill, named `YYYY-MM-DD-[company]-ic-feedback-dd-guide.md`, saved to deals/[Company]/3-ic-feedback/.
- Never fabricate an IC concern or an earlier flag. Synthesize only what is in the discussion notes and the earlier documents. If an earlier document is missing, say that its questions could not be included.
