The due-diligence (DD) area, in six sub-folders:

- `1-market-competitive/` holds the market analysis and the competition analysis (from Pre-IC onward), their DD-refined versions, and market material from the data room.
- `2-management/` holds the management DD write-up and team material.
- `3-product-tech/` holds product and technology material: architecture notes, stack review, demo observations.
- `4-financial/` holds the company's financial model, KPI files and the financial DD write-up.
- `5-legal/` holds the cap table, corporate documents, investment instruments, and the legal DD outputs.
- `6-interviews/` holds every DD call note: team interviews, investor references, client references and expert reviews. Keep the files flat. A hint of who the call was with, and a date, in the file name helps but is not required.

You can drop a full, unsorted data room straight into `4-dd/` and ask for it to be organized. The data-room-organizer sorts each file into the right sub-folder and reports every move.

Impact material belongs in the `1-impact/` stage folder, not here.
