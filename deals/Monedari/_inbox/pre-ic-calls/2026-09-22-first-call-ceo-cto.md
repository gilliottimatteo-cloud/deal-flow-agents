## Summary

Mariana and Diego walked through the Monedari seed deck. Monedari lets neighborhood shops in Mexico (and a pilot in Bogota) take card payments on their own Android phone, plus free QR transfers, with next-day payouts. They are raising USD 3M on a post-money SAFE at a USD 12M cap and have USD 1.7M committed. They asked Example Ventures for USD 500K. Tomas asked about acquirer dependence, the cash advance product and retention. Both sides agreed to continue; the team will share a data room.

## Discussion points

**Origin and team**
- Mariana spent seven years selling to tienditas; the idea came from shop owners asking her route reps whether they "had the card thing". Diego built the payment core himself.
- A third co-founder, Andres P., left in August 2024. Mariana: "He wanted something less intense, it was friendly." Andres still holds 4% of the company. Tomas asked whether that is vested; Mariana believes so and said the separation agreement will be in the data room.
- No CFO. Finance is handled by a part-time contractor. Mariana said a finance lead is "on the hiring plan for after the round".

**Payments and the acquirer**
- All card processing runs through one acquiring bank, under an aggregator agreement. Diego: "They have been great partners, we are their biggest tap-to-phone client." The agreement renews in June 2027.
- Tomas asked what happens if the bank changes terms. Diego said a second acquirer is part of the use of funds (25% of the round, together with the risk engine) and that conversations "are advanced".
- Colombia is QR only for now. Card acceptance there needs a local acquirer.

**Adelanto (cash advance)**
- Pilot since February 2026. 640 advances so far. Repaid as a share of daily card sales.
- Funded from Monedari's own balance sheet, from the equity raised. Diego: "We wanted to learn the risk before bringing in a lender."
- Lucia asked about losses. Mariana said "around 7% of what we lent, and coming down every month". Detailed file to come.
- Nobody on the team has a credit or risk background. The risk rules are written by Diego and the data engineer.

**Traction and retention**
- Six-month retention: 62% for the 2025 cohorts, 71% for the first-half 2026 cohorts. Tomas asked whether the improvement comes from the wholesaler channel, which grew in 2026. Mariana: "Partly, yes, but the product also got better." She will send the cohorts split by channel.
- Wholesalers brought 46% of new merchants this year.

**Cash and the round**
- Cash at end of September: about USD 1.05M. Net burn about USD 150K a month. Runway about seven months.
- Lucia asked whether the USD 1.7M is in the bank. Mariana: the round runs as a rolling close. The lead fund wired its USD 1.2M in May and the angels wired their USD 0.5M in July and August, so the USD 1.05M already includes that money.
- Mariana said April was "very tight" before the lead's money arrived.
